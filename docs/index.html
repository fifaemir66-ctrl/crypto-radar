How it works

  1. Trend first. Only markets in an uptrend (daily and weekly) are considered. Everything else is AVOID.
  2. Wait for the zone. In an uptrend, price is WATCH until it pulls back into a buy zone where several supports overlap.
  3. Wait for the trigger. In the zone it becomes GET READY. A daily close with a hammer, bullish engulfing or liquidity sweep turns it into a BUY SIGNAL, with entry, stop, target and position size.

Signals are decided once a day on the daily close (crypto 00:00 UTC, stocks at the US close), so they don't flip back and forth during the day.
